About

Circuits, code, and the bugs in between.

I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIIT Ranchi, alongside a BS in Data Science and AI at IIT Madras. The pairing is deliberate. ECE gives me the foundations of circuits, signals, and microcontroller architecture, while the data science degree gives me the tools to make hardware smarter.

My work centers on edge AI: getting intelligence to run on constrained devices instead of depending on the cloud. That means thinking hard about memory budgets, power, latency, and the trade-offs between model capability and what a microcontroller can realistically handle.

I’m happiest at the low level, with a logic analyzer in hand and a misbehaving bus to track down. Chasing a stray bit through an SPI transaction or a corrupted buffer teaches you how systems really work, and that habit of understanding things from the ground up shapes everything I build.
